## Simple Lists (Default theme)

Simple unordered list:

 • Coffee
 • Tea
 • Milk

Nested unordered lists (showing depth rotation):

 • Level 1 - disc marker
   ▪ Level 2 - dash marker
     ◦ Level 3 - arrow marker
       • Level 4 - rotates back to disc

Simple ordered list:

 1. Coffee
 2. Tea
 3. Milk

Nested ordered lists (showing depth rotation):

 1. Level 1 - numeric (1, 2, 3)
    I. Level 2 - Roman (I, II, III)
       A. Level 3 - Uppercase letters (A, B, C)
          i. Level 4 - lowercase roman (i, ii, iii)
             a. Level 5 - lowercase letters (a, b, c)
