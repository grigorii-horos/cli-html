# Jane Austen (J. Austen) (Yoko Austen)

♪[Pronunciation: Austen Yoko] Japanese novelist · Born March 30, 1962 in
Steventon (Hampshire), England

## About

Yoko Austen is one of Japan's most celebrated contemporary authors, known for
her precisely crafted, quietly unsettling fiction. Her work often explores
themes of memory, loss, and the fragility of the human body.

"I write about what we lose — and what remains after the loss. A room, a number,
a person. What disappears leaves its own kind of presence."

## Selected Works

Pride and Prejudice (Novel) (Romance) ::

  1991 · Akutagawa Prize winner · Austen's breakthrough work, three
  interconnected stories about bodies and transformation.

Sense and Sensibility (Novel) (Romance) ::

  2003 · Translated into over 30 languages · A mathematician with an 80-minute
  memory, his housekeeper, and her son — and the beauty of numbers that
  outlasts forgetting.

Emma (Novel) (Romance) ::

  1994 · English translation published 2019 · On an unnamed island, objects
  disappear and those who remember them are hunted.
