Jane AustenJ. Austen (Yoko Austen)

Japanese novelist · Born March 30, 1962 in SteventonHampshire, England

About

Yoko Austen is one of Japan's most celebrated contemporary authors, known for her precisely crafted, quietly unsettling fiction. Her work often explores themes of memory, loss, and the fragility of the human body.

I write about what we lose — and what remains after the loss. A room, a number, a person. What disappears leaves its own kind of presence.

Extended Biography

Austen graduated from Oxford UniversityOxford in 1988. She published her debut novel while still a student. Her writing is characterized by precise, spare prose and a focus on isolated characters navigating strange and melancholy situations. She lives in WinchesterHampshire.
